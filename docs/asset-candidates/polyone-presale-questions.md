## Topology and appearance

Please provide un-subdivided control-mesh wireframes and neutral shaded close-ups of:

- Face: front, strict side and 45 degrees, including eyelids, nose underside, lips, jaw and ears.
- Hands: palm, back, finger webs, knuckle loops, thumb base and wrist/forearm connection. Please show how nails and nail beds are modeled.
- Shoulders: clavicle, deltoid, chest/back connection and axilla, plus elbows.
- Any included clothing around the armhole. If no sleeves are included, please confirm that limitation.

Please state actual vertex/face counts, whether the editable base is all quads, and whether hands/body are continuous or separate components. Intentional component boundaries are fine if identified.

## Deformation

Could you provide short continuous videos, or clear pose sequences, using the supplied rig and weights with neutral lighting, no effects and visible wireframe overlays where useful?

- Arms naturally down; forward raises at 45, 90 and 120 degrees.
- Side raises at 45, 90 and 120 degrees; upper-arm internal/external rotation.
- Elbow flexion and forearm/wrist rotation.
- Open hand, half fist and full fist, showing palm and back.
- Thumb opposition to the index and middle fingers, showing the thumb base and contact area.

Please include transitions, not only selected end poses. We need to assess shoulder volume, axilla continuity, finger compression and self-intersections. Please identify whether these demonstrations use additional corrective work absent from the sold package.

## Rig and skinning

- Are the skeleton and skin weights included in both Blender and FBX? Which software versions are supported?
- What are the total/deform bone counts and maximum influences per vertex?
- Does the rig rely on dual-quaternion skinning, B-bones, constraints, drivers, add-ons, lattices or corrective smoothing?
- Are corrective shapes and a facial rig included? Can their results be baked to ordinary bone transforms and morph-weight animation?
- Can you show a linear-skinning export with at most four influences per vertex, especially at shoulders and thumbs? Has the exported result actually been tested?

- If available, could you provide an explicitly licensed evaluation file or a screen recording of the relevant source inspection? We are not requesting unauthorized access to paid files.
